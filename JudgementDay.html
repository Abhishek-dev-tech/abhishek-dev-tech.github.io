Judgement Day

About

Judgment Day is a psychological horror puzzle-adventure game.

Detective Roger Morgan is trapped in a twisted version of his partner Jack Clark’s home. Each puzzle he solves unravels dark secrets, distorting reality and blurring the line between friend and foe, forcing him to question everything.

Project Info

  • Role: Game Programming
  • Team Size: 10
  • Time Frame: 1 year and 6 months
  • Engine: Unreal Engine 5

My Role

As a Game Programmer, I was responsible for designing and implementing the core gameplay systems that drove player interaction within the game.

I collaborated closely with animation and design workflows to ensure gameplay mechanics felt intuitive and responsive, while maintaining clean and maintainable code architecture.


What I Learned

This project helped me improve my understanding of gameplay programming in Unreal Engine 5 using C++. I learned how to build reusable gameplay systems, handle player interactions, and connect gameplay logic with animations.
